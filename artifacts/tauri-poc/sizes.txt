=== Full distribution (different roles) ===
Tauri release binary only: 17115248 bytes (~17 MiB)
Tauri AppImage (bundles WebKit/GTK via linuxdeploy): 86301176 bytes (~82 MiB)

AppImage is large because it ships WebKitGTK, GTK3, and dependencies for distros without guaranteed system WebKit.
Lean tar.xz is viable on Arch/Ubuntu when webkit2gtk-4.1 + GTK3 are installed (same assumption as many WebKit apps).
